Mid-run pickup — the story is long done. History Mode, Special Story, every
S-rank, all six Survival courses and Shrewd Collector are already banked from
the November 2023 run, so this is pure cleanup: 8 trophies, none missable,
nothing skill-gated.

Two of them are freebies I somehow walked past — **Customize and win!** and
**League Champ** are maybe 20 minutes combined. **Ninja Gathering**,
**Multi-Title Holder** and **Rich in Knowledge** are the same grind wearing
three hats: rotate themed trios of fresh characters through Ultimate Survival
team matches to Proficiency 5, and all three counters climb together.

The whole platinum then comes down to **Supreme Domain** — five characters at
Proficiency 30. Lock in the five on the first night and never rotate them out.
Realistically 15–20 hours from here.
