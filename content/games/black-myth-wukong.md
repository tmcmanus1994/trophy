Mid-run pickup, and a good one: the story is fully done — both endings, Erlang
included — so nothing narrative is left. What's left is the part the game hides
behind a second cycle. Fourteen trophies remain and eleven of them are
collection or crafting, which means the honest plan is: finish every free-roam
sweep I can still reach (curios, seeds, meditation spots, journal), then commit
to New Game+ and craft out the rest. The one real trap left is **Portraits
Perfected** — three journal entries are chapter-locked and free-roam won't give
them back, so those get logged on the NG+ pass, chapter by chapter, before
anything else.
