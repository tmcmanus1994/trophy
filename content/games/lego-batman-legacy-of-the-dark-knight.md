Mid-run pickup: story's done and dusted, so this is a pure cleanup grind — 38 of
52 already in the bag, 14 left, and every one of them is a counter. Nothing is
missable in this game, which means the only real enemy is volume: 41 suits, 15
vehicles, 21 WayneTech caches, 14 red bricks, 25 Bat-Symbols from scratch, and
13 puzzle-room sets. Priority order is WayneTech caches first, since they gate a
long ladder of suits and vehicles, then puzzle rooms, then let the stud purchases
fall out of the studs you pick up on the way. The Bat-Symbols are the one thing
the towers won't mark for you, so those need a locations guide.
