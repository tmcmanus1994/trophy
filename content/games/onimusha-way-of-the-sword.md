Fresh start — nothing earned yet. I'm playing the story blind on Action and
enjoying it; the only habits I'm keeping are buying the three Oni Vision
skills first, hoarding consumables for Carnage, talking to every townsperson
I save, and chipping at the five combat counters that have no practice drill.
The real trophy work starts at the Point of No Return before "The Clash at
Rendaino": manual save, full cleanup, finish the story, NG+ on Carnage, then
Boss Rematch to close it out.
