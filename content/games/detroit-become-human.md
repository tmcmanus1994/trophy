December sprint, so this is pure cleanup. Six left and they split cleanly:
BOOKWORM and THESE ARE OUR STORIES are menu work I can knock out from chapter
select, and the other four all live on the same hostile machine-Connor run.

Kara captured instead of clean at the border, and Connor dying at all ten
opportunities — one nasty playthrough that does all three at once. Then
DETROIT MASTER falls out the far end.
